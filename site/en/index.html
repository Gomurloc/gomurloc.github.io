필소굿

Spirit, lend me your beat.

A one-button cyber-shaman rhythm game.

Jackdoo, a cyborg shaman about to be repossessed for a missed installment, borrows your beat — you’re the offscreen spirit — to throw a ritual party for a clown-faced water ghost who drowned holding a crashed stock, and send him on his way. Characters play a rhythm; you answer with a single button across a run of minigames. Neon cyber-city meets Korean shamanism and stock-forum slang in a black comedy.

Salvage it out there. Sell it back home.

Top Excellence Award (최우수상), 2026 Future Game Jam

A century after the sky died, people live on orbital stations, and the wrecks circling Earth are currency. As an indebted salvager, latch onto wrecks, crack open cargo bays, then fly home to sell your haul and upgrade your ship for higher orbits. But every bay you open makes noise — and the Sweepers hunt noise.

STUDIO

KO Studio is the game studio of designer and sound designer Ko Yujun, and the name is Ko Yujun’s own. Ko is the family name, and the paper figure in the intro is ㅇ and ㅈ, the Korean initials of Yujun. Knock it down and it becomes K·O.

That’s the kind of game KO Studio makes: one that flips your expectations in a single blow.

Right now, Ko Yujun is building two games together with a fellow developer and an artist.
